CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Can you get us into Google's top 3?

We can't promise a rank; anyone who does is guessing. From month 2 we work on what Google rewards: the right categories, replies to every review, weekly posts and photos, and consistent listings. You see where you rank around your clinic every month.

Do you guarantee patients?

No. Nobody honest can. We commit to the work: 3 ads, setup, daily reports and full transparency on numbers. Enquiries, bookings and revenue depend on several factors, including your offer and follow-up.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly on your own card, CA$15–30/day. We never handle your ad money.

Do I need a website?

No. Enquiries come through an Instagram/Facebook form and are sent to your front desk.

Is there a contract?

No. After 14 days you decide.

Which clinics do you work with?

Physiotherapy and aesthetics clinics (laser, skin, med spa), starting with Brampton and Mississauga, then the wider GTA. We still talk to other clinic types case by case.

Will patients' data be safe?

We limit access to you, us and the service providers needed to deliver enquiries. No system is risk-free. Each clinic's patient lead form needs its own privacy notice; see our Privacy Policy for how these differ.

Can you follow medical advertising rules?

Yes. We avoid misleading claims and before/after promises, and follow Meta's health ad policies. We check creative with you before launch; professional and treatment-specific restrictions may also apply.

Do the ads follow Ontario college advertising rules?

Yes. We follow your regulator’s rules, for example the College of Physiotherapists of Ontario, or the CPSO for physician-led clinics. The ads stay factual: no superiority terms like “best” or “state of the art”, no unverifiable claims, nothing that plays on fear, and testimonials or review requests only where your college allows them. You approve every ad before it runs.

Where are you based?

Siddharth runs Pinlyft remotely and works Toronto mornings, Mon–Fri 9am–1pm ET. Your ads run on your own Meta ad account in your name, so you see every dollar in real time.

How are enquiries delivered?

Each enquiry (name, phone, treatment interest) is sent to your front desk by email or text the same day, plus a daily summary. Calling back within an hour makes the biggest difference.
