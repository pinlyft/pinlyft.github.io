CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Do you guarantee jobs?

No. Nobody honest can. We commit to the work: 3 ads, setup, daily reports and full transparency on numbers. Booked jobs depend on your price, response speed and service.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly on your own card, CA$20–40/day. We never handle your ad money.

Do the ads mention rebates?

Only factually. Rebate amounts depend on the Home Renovation Savings Program's rules and the homeowner's eligibility, so the ads never promise an amount. If you're a participating contractor, we can say so; if not, we leave rebates out.

What about TSSA and licensing?

Your licensing stays your business; we don't make claims about it unless you give us the exact wording.

Will I get serious homeowners?

The form asks what they need, their city and timing, so you can call the serious ones first. Calling back within an hour makes the biggest difference.

Do I need a website?

No. Quote requests come through an Instagram/Facebook form and are sent to your phone.

Is there a contract?

No. After 14 days you decide.

Where are you based?

Siddharth runs Pinlyft remotely and works Toronto mornings, Mon–Fri 9am–1pm ET. Your ads run on your own Meta ad account in your name, so you see every dollar in real time.
