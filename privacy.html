YOUR INFORMATION, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pinlyft is a founder-led Meta advertising agency operated by Siddharth in Delhi NCR, India. This notice covers enquiries about Pinlyft's services made through this website, our own Meta lead forms, WhatsApp, phone or email. It is intended for adult clinic owners, doctors and authorised managers, not patients.

1. What we collect

We collect the information you choose to provide: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, clinic name and city/area. Our website form also asks for clinic type, whether you run ads, your role, how you heard about us and consent to contact. With a form submission we also receive the campaign source of the link you used and the page you landed on (see section 8). If you email or message us, we also receive your email address or messaging profile and the contents of your communication. Please do not submit patient records, diagnoses, photographs or other medical information.

Our hosting and form providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, request time and security logs to operate their services and prevent abuse.

2. Why we use it

We use your enquiry information to respond, assess whether our service suits your clinic, discuss the 14-Day Enquiry Sprint, arrange a call and follow up about this service by WhatsApp or phone with your consent. We also use necessary information to manage enquiries, prevent spam and record your contact preferences. We do not sell your personal data or use your enquiry as permission for unrelated marketing.

3. Consent and your choices

Submitting the website form requires your agreement to service-related WhatsApp/phone contact and this notice. Our own Meta lead forms should present the same purpose and consent before submission. You can withdraw consent or ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying to our message or emailing us. Withdrawal does not undo processing that already occurred lawfully.

4. Who receives your information

Siddharth and any authorised support acting under confidentiality may access information needed to handle your enquiry. We use GitHub Pages for hosting, Formspree for form delivery, email services for notifications and WhatsApp/Meta when you contact us there or submit our Meta lead forms. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices and may process it outside India. We disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect lawful rights. We do not share your details with unrelated clinics or sell leads to others.

Provider notices: GitHub, Formspree, WhatsApp and Meta.

5. How long we keep it

We retain service-enquiry personal data for up to 12 months from your most recent enquiry or service-related interaction, then delete or anonymise it unless a shorter period is requested or a longer period is required by law. This includes copies we control in email, lead lists and messaging accounts. Providers may maintain independent security logs or backup copies under their own retention rules. If you become a client, legally required accounting or service records may have different retention periods, explained when relevant.

6. Security

We use access restrictions, account security measures and HTTPS-enabled service providers to protect information. We limit collection and access to what is needed for the service. No online service can be completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive medical information through these channels.

7. Access, correction, deletion and complaints

To request access to information we hold, correct details, withdraw consent, request deletion or raise a privacy concern, contact Siddharth at our privacy contact. Use the subject “Privacy request” and identify the phone number or email used for your enquiry. We may request proportionate verification before acting; do not send identity documents unless necessary and requested. We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 working days and resolve them within 30 days, subject to applicable law.

This notice is framed with reference to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as applicable and brought into force. Available statutory rights and complaint routes depend on the provisions in force. You may use applicable grievance and regulatory mechanisms where available.

8. Cookies, analytics and tracking

Analytics (only if you accept). When you click “Accept analytics”, this site loads Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. They tell us which pages and links are used, and Clarity records anonymised page interactions such as clicks and scrolling so we can fix confusing parts of the page. They use cookies and similar storage and process technical data such as your IP address, device and browser. Form fields you type are masked by Clarity by default. If you click “No thanks”, neither tool loads. Your choice is saved in this browser; to change it, clear this site’s data in your browser.

Where you came from. To know which of our posts, messages or ads led to an application, the site remembers the campaign tags in the link you used (such as utm_source), the page you first landed on and the referring website, in your browser’s storage. This is sent to us only if you submit the application form, together with your answer to “How did you hear about Pinlyft?”.

Advertising tracking. Meta Pixel is not active. If we turn it on, it will load only after you accept, and we will update this notice first.

Other services. Google Fonts loads typefaces, so your browser connects to Google, which may receive your IP address and technical request details. Formspree receives what you submit in the form. Opening Instagram or WhatsApp takes you to those services under their own notices.

9. Patient enquiries in clinic campaigns

This policy covers clinic owners enquiring about Pinlyft, including via Pinlyft's own Meta lead forms. It is not a replacement for a clinic's patient privacy notice. For campaigns run on a clinic's behalf, the clinic determines the enquiry purpose and should provide a clinic-specific notice identifying the clinic, requested fields, relevant providers, contact details and rights. Pinlyft processes those leads on the clinic's instructions under agreed safeguards; access is limited to the clinic, Pinlyft and necessary delivery providers. Do not use this agency-owner form to collect patient or medical details.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as our service or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version. Pinlyft, Delhi NCR, India. Privacy contact: Siddharth, contact us. Working hours: Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm IST.
