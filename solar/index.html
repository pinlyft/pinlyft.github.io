CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you ask.

Do you guarantee installations?

No. Nobody honest can. We commit to the work: 3 ads, setup, daily reports and full transparency on numbers. Site visits and sales depend on your price, follow-up speed and service.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly on your own card, ₹300–500/day. We never handle your ad money.

Do the ads talk about the subsidy?

Yes, factually. The PM Surya Ghar subsidy is decided by the government, not by us or you, so the ads never promise an amount or “free electricity”. They point people to check eligibility with you.

Do I need to be a registered PM Surya Ghar vendor?

Not to work with us, but if the ads mention the subsidy, you must be registered for the state where you install. We check this with you before launch.

Will I get homeowners or tyre-kickers?

Some enquiries will be early-stage; that is normal for solar. The form asks about roof type, monthly bill and city, so you can call the serious ones first. Calling back within an hour makes the biggest difference.

Do I need a website?

No. Enquiries come through an Instagram/Facebook form and are sent to your phone.

Is there a contract?

No. After 14 days you decide.

Where are you based?

Delhi NCR. Your ads run on your own Meta ad account in your name, so you see every rupee in real time.
